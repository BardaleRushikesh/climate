Climate Change and Sustainable living

What is Climate Change?

  • Climate change is a long-term change in the average weather patterns that have come to define Earth's local, regional and global climates. These changes have a broad range of observed effects that are synonymous with the term.

  • Change is one of the most pressing issues facing our planet today. Rising global temperatures, extreme weather conditions, and environmental degradation are just a few of the consequences of human activities that contribute to climate change.

  • Present-day climate change includes both global warming—the ongoing increase in global average temperature—and its wider effects on Earth’s climate system.

Impact of Climate change

1.Environmental Impacts
The environmental effects of climate change are broad and far-reaching, affecting oceans, ice, and weather. Changes may occur gradually or rapidly. Evidence for these effects comes from studying climate change in the past, from modelling, and from modern observations.
2.Nature and Wildlife
Recent warming has driven many terrestrial and freshwater species poleward and towards higher altitudes.For instance, the range of hundreds of North American birds has shifted northward at an average rate of 1.5 km/year over the past 55 years.Higher atmospheric CO2 levels and an extended growing season have resulted in global greening. However, this greening is not uniform. Some regions have experienced browning, or vegetation loss, due to extreme events like droughts.
3.Health and Food
The World Health Organization calls climate change one of the biggest threats to global health in the 21st century. Scientists have warned about the irreversible harms it poses. Extreme weather events affect public health, food and water security and Temperature extremes lead to increased illness and death.

Causes of Climate Change

  1. Burning of fossil fuels
  2. Deforestation
  3. Industrial processes
  4. Agriculture

Sustainable Living

Sustainable living is a lifestyle that aims to reduce one's environmental impact, in ways that are sustainable both for the Earth and for the individual. It involves making conscious choices to minimize the use of Earth's resources and reduce waste. Some ways to live sustainably include:

  • Reducing, reusing, and recycling
  • Conserving energy and water
  • Supporting local and sustainable businesses
  • Eating a plant-based diet
  • Using alternative modes of transportation
